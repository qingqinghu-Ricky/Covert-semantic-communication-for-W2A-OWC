# Dataset and pretrained asset terms

UAM contains images collected from SUIM, WebUOT-1M, COU and public image
platforms, including Wikimedia Commons, with annotations prepared by the paper
authors. **Original images remain subject to their respective source licenses
and attribution requirements.**

Third-party code and initialization assets retain their original terms; see
NOTICE.md and checkpoints/manifest.json. The ResNet18 ImageNet initialization
comes from torchvision.
